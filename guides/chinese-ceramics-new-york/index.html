Chinese ceramics in New York

New York has been a centre for Chinese art for over a century, and it is still one of the best places in the world to buy Chinese ceramics. We are a collector-run business based in New York, selling Song, Yuan and earlier pieces, and this page shows what we currently have for sale.

Why New York

New York's Chinese art market goes back to the great collections of the early 20th century, and the city still hosts the major auction houses, museums and dealers. Buying in New York means buying in a market with deep expertise, and with the Metropolitan Museum's Asian art collection a subway ride away, it is also one of the best places to learn.

What we sell

Our collection focuses on Song and Yuan ceramics: Longquan celadon, Jingdezhen qingbai, Cizhou ware, alongside earlier pottery and bronze. Prices run from $400 to $4,100, and every piece is described in full, with its condition and provenance. We are collectors ourselves, and we sell the way we would want to buy: honestly, and with a guarantee.

Viewing in New York

We are based in Manhattan. If you are in New York and would like to see a piece in person before you buy, write to us and we will arrange it. Seeing a ceramic in daylight, holding it, and turning it over tells you more than any photograph.

Shipping from New York

We ship from New York, fully insured, within 3 business days of payment. Shipping within the United States is free, and international shipping is free on pieces of $2,500 or more. If you are buying from overseas, we can also advise on import rules for your country.
