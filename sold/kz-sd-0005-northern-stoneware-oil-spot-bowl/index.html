Northern stoneware “oil spot” bowl

黑釉油滴盌

Stock no. KZ-SD-0005

Yuan dynasty (1279–1368)
Diameter 7¼ in. (18.4 cm)

Sold October 2026

View the collection

Notes
The domical bowl on a short circular foot, the black glaze suffused with brown effusions of various sizes.
Provenance
A European private collection; Sotheby's Paris, 21 December 2023, lot 108 (part lot); Waddington's, Toronto, 11 April 2024, lot 175, as Yuan dynasty.

Dark glazed bowls were made for whisked tea, where the white froth showed best against a black ground. In the “oil spot” glaze, iron in the glaze separates during firing into brown spots of many sizes, like drops of oil on water.
