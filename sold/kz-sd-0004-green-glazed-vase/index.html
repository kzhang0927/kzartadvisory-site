Green glazed vase

Northern Dynasties (386–581)

Sold October 2026

View the collection

Notes
With bulbous body, waisted neck, cupped lip, and four lug handles at the shoulder (two doubled), covered in a pale green glaze.

Northern Dynasties potters were among the first in China to cover stoneware in a green glaze, the early steps toward celadon. A closely related vase is published in Mino, Ice and Green Clouds: Traditions of Chinese Celadon, cat. no. 37.
