Eastern Tibetan inlaid iron censer

藏式鐵錯銀方爐

Stock no. KZ-SD-0003

Late Ming to early Qing dynasty (17th century)
Height 10 × Length 10½ × Width 6½ in. (25.5 × 26.8 × 16.5 cm)

Sold September 2026

View the collection

Notes
Of rectangular form raised on angular feet, with loose ring handles to the sides and a slightly domed cover with angular finial, applied shaped bosses and panels, variously inlaid with fret diaper, auspicious motifs and symbols; parcel-gilt bats at the rim.
Provenance
Christie's London, 12 November 2002, lot 28; ex private English collection; Christie's Paris, 13 June 2025, lot 670.

This vessel belongs to a small group of unusual wrought-iron vessels assembled from pieced elements and inlaid in silver and gold, attributed to makers in Kham, on the border between Tibet and western China.
