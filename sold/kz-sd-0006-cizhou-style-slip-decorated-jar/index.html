Cizhou-style slip-decorated jar

磁州窯花卉紋大罐

Stock no. KZ-SD-0006

Yuan dynasty (1279–1368)
Height 11½ × Diameter 12½ in. (29.2 × 31.8 cm)

Sold October 2026

View the collection

Notes
Of bulbous form with short neck, decorated with stylized lotus motifs in iron brown against a cream slip.
Provenance
Property of an international collector, Washington, D.C.; Doyle, New York, 19 March 2024, lot 151, as Yuan dynasty.

Cizhou potters painted freely in iron brown on a cream slip ground, a decoration as bold and quick as ink painting. Lotus scrolls are among the most characteristic Cizhou motifs.
