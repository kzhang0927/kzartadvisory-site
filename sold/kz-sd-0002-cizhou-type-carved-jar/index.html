Notes
Of broad bulbous form, with low rounded lip, the body tapering to a low circular foot; the shoulder carved through the thick brown-black glaze with stylized foliage.

Cizhou wares were the everyday stonewares of northern China, made at many kilns. The boldest pieces were carved through a dark glaze to the pale body beneath, a technique that suits large jars.
