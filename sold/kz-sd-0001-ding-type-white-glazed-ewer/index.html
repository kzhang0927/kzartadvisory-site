Ding-type white glazed ewer

仿定窯白釉執壺

Stock no. KZ-SD-0001

Northern Song dynasty (960–1127)
Height 7½ in. (19 cm)

Sold September 2026

View the collection

Notes
Of bulbous form with tall flared neck, flared foot, ridged and indented loop handle, and slender curved spout.

White glazed wares in the manner of Ding, the great Song dynasty kilns of Dingzhou in Hebei, are prized for thin bodies and an ivory tone. Ewers of this form held wine.
