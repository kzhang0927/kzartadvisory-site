About

I am a collector of historic objects, drawn above all to medieval and ancient pieces for the history they carry. To me, an object is a snapshot in time: a record of the taste, skill and ideals of people who lived long ago.

My particular interests are Goryeo celadon, functional objects made to be used, such as scholar's tools, and inscribed objects. I see myself as a temporary steward of these pieces. Selling is how they pass to the next collector who will care for them.

Our guarantee

Every piece is guaranteed to be as described, with no time limit. If it is ever shown not to be, we refund the full price and shipping.
