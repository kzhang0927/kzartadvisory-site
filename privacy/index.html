Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit this website, buy from us or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you buy a piece, our payment provider collects your name, email address, shipping address and payment details on our behalf. We receive your name, contact details and shipping address, but never your full card number.

When you email us, we keep your message and email address so that we can reply.

How we use it

We use your information only to process and ship your order, answer your questions, provide receipts and meet our legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask to receive them.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with our payment provider (to take payment), shipping carriers and insurers (to deliver and insure your parcel), and our accountant or tax authorities where the law requires it.

Cookies and your cart

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping cart is stored only in your own browser, and you can clear it at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually seven years. We delete other correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, unless we are required to keep it by law. Write to kzartadvisory@gmail.com.
