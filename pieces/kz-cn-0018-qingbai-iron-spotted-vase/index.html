Qingbai wares decorated with clusters of iron-brown spots were popular in the Yuan dynasty. Dabbed on before glazing, the spots fire to a dark, rich brown against the pale bluish-white glaze. Small vases like this were made in large numbers, many for export across Southeast Asia.
