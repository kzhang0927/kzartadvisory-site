Octagonal dishes are among the more refined Goryeo forms. Each side panel is framed in white inlay and holds a chrysanthemum spray inlaid in white and black slip. The chrysanthemum, a symbol of longevity, was one of the favourite motifs of Goryeo inlaid celadon.
